Engineering Services

CEA-UFMG acts as a strategic innovation partner for aerospace, defense, and advanced air mobility companies, bringing proven expertise in high-performance experimental aircraft.

[SERVICE 01 · AERODYNAMICS]

High-Fidelity RANS CFD & Wind Tunnel Testing

Natural Laminar Flow (NLF) airfoil optimization, parasite drag minimization, and subsonic wind tunnel boundary layer characterization.

RANS k-ω SST CFD NLF LAMINAR FLOW WIND TUNNEL TESTING
[SERVICE 02 · STRUCTURES]

FEA Analysis & Composite Sandwich Construction

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) structural sizing, torsional stiffness calculation, flutter prevention, and carbon-fiber vacuum infusion using Nomex honeycomb sandwich cores.

FINITE ELEMENT ANALYSIS (FEA) FLUTTER SUPPRESSION NOMEX HONEYCOMB CORES
[SERVICE 03 · FLIGHT TESTING]

Flight Test Campaigns & CAN-Bus Telemetry

Development of flight test data acquisition systems and airborne inertial units (IMU/multi-hole pitot sensing) for real-time telemetry up to 100 Hz.

100 Hz TELEMETRY CAN-BUS NETWORK AIR DATA CALIBRATION
[SERVICE 04 · AIRWORTHINESS]

Airworthiness & Fleet Engineering Consulting

Aeronautical engineering consulting in reliability, MSG-3 fleet maintenance programs, technical documentation, and civil aviation regulatory support.

MSG-3 METHODOLOGY FLEET MANAGEMENT ANAC / CIVIL AVIATION

Full-Scale Experimental Aircraft Engineering

Turnkey aircraft conceptual design, detailed CAD/CAM, structural prototyping, and flight test certification (e.g. Aeromot twin-engine ethanol aircraft).

CONCEPT TO FLIGHT SUSTAINABLE PROPULSION FAI CERTIFICATION
