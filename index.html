Aeronautical Heritage · Est. 1963

Engineering Through Real Aircraft

Founded by Professor Cláudio Pinto de Barros at the Federal University of Minas Gerais, CEA developed a practical approach to engineering education: connect design decisions with construction, experiments and flight experience, and pass that knowledge to the next generation.

Requirements and calculations guide a design, workshop decisions turn it into hardware, and measurements test the assumptions. Technical knowledge grows through that continuous connection, preparing engineers capable of conceiving, fabricating and flight-validating complete aeronautical systems.

Engineering Education · The Akaflieg Tradition

Projects That Connect Generations

At CEA, engineering education moves beyond calculations on paper. Drawing upon the German Akafliegs tradition, students learn to formulate operational missions, compare design alternatives, and justify their engineering choices through physical evidence and flight experience.

01

Mission Formulation & Sizing

Translating aerodynamic requirements and weight budgets into robust preliminary aircraft configurations.

02

Workshop Craftsmanship & Prototyping

Working directly with composite materials, bonded joints, and structural testing in the university laboratory.

03

Flight Testing & Evidence

Connecting real-time airborne telemetry, sensor calibration, and handling evaluations with theoretical predictions.

Institutional Cooperation · Advanced Aerospace

Research & Development Collaboration

In September 2024, Aeromot and the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG) announced an institutional research and technological development agreement. Grounded in CEA's cumulative experience in aeronautical design and flight testing, the partnership bridges university research with the aerospace industry.

The joint effort provides an institutional framework for aeronautical studies and the investigation of technologies for regional air mobility, engaging professors, researchers, and engineers in applied aerospace challenges.

01

Joint Research Framework

Establishing collaborative research channels between university researchers and industry aerospace teams.

02

Aeronautical Technology Studies

Investigating concepts for regional air mobility and advanced light aircraft development.

03

Knowledge & Capability Development

Strengthening national aeronautical engineering expertise through applied research projects.
