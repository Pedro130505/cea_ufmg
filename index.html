About Us

The "Learning by Doing" Tradition

The Center for Aeronautical Studies (CEA) is a state-of-the-art research laboratory within the Department of Mechanical Engineering at UFMG School of Engineering. Founded in 1963 by the visionary Professor Cláudio Pinto de Barros, CEA pioneered the adoption of the German Akaflieg academic model in Brazil.

Here, engineering students do not just absorb classroom theory; they participate directly in all phases of experimental aircraft creation: aerodynamic design, complex FEA structural analysis, advanced composite manufacturing (carbon fiber and Nomex aramid honeycomb cores), avionics and propulsion system integration, and flight test campaigns.

This hands-on philosophy produces highly qualified engineers sought after globally, with alumni currently leading teams at Embraer, Boeing, Airbus, and Red Bull Air Race teams.
